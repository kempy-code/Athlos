Privacy policy

Last updated 22 August 2026

Data Athlos stores

Account details, athlete profile answers, generated plans, workout logs, readiness check-ins, health data you choose to import, goals and coach messages.

Why it is used

To provide personalised training, synchronize your account, calculate progress and respond through the AI Coach.

Health information

Health and training information is sensitive. Athlos only processes information you submit or explicitly authorize. Athlos does not provide diagnosis or emergency medical services.

Control

You can download your data or permanently delete your account from Account settings. Connected-service authorization can be revoked with the relevant provider.

Production notice

Before public launch, replace this project policy with a jurisdiction-appropriate policy identifying the operator, processors, retention periods and contact details.
